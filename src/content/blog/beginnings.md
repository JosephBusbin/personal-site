### The intent of the blog from the beginning
It is not often that I like to share my thoughts.
Don’t expect them to be formal pieces, to have particularly consistent
topics choice or to be a definitive judgment on any issue. Mostly
they will be lighthearted contemplations of the many topics that I
ponder about. Please read to enjoy.

### Initial Goals
This site started as a project in the summer with a few purposes: 
1. Do something new and cool
2. Build something
3. Have a tangible benefit to my career in someway
4. Get a feeling for web design
5. Experiment with AI

The original set of purposes is fulfilled as is clear
in the construction and operation of this website.
I don’t have great expectation for what it will evolve into, 
and I was surprised that I wanted to add a blog to an otherwise 
career focused site. I may remove the blog portion or put is on 
a separate platform one day, but it can live here for now. 

### Welcome
I can’t particularly imagine what I will post
about that others would be interested in reading, 
but maybe such things are expressions meant for the 
artist and not the audience. 
So it has it’s own purpose in that way. 

Welcome and Happy reading,

Joseph Busbin
