This is a sample research entry — delete or overwrite it.

## Structure

Everything above the `---` divider is metadata; everything below is the
write-up. The `type` field must be one of `academic`, `informal`, or `project`,
and it drives the label shown on the index page.

Use `venue` for a journal, conference, or course context, `authors` for a
byline, and `links` for anything external — a PDF, a DOI, a repository, slides.
Each link needs a `label` and a valid `href`.

## Body

Below the frontmatter, write as much or as little as you want. For work that
lives primarily as a PDF, a few paragraphs of context plus the link is plenty.
For a project write-up with no paper behind it, this section carries the whole
thing.
